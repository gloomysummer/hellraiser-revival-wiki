The direct answer: **No redeemable text promo codes exist for Hellraiser: Revival.** There is no code redemption screen, text field, or developer voucher system in the game. All downloadable content, pre-order weapons, and Deluxe Edition cosmetics unlock automatically through your digital storefront entitlement.

## How Pre-Order & Deluxe Edition Rewards Actually Unlock

If you purchased the Deluxe Edition, Collector's Edition, or pre-ordered the standard game on Steam, PlayStation 5, or Xbox Series X|S, your bonus items do not require typing any string:

1. **License Verification**: Launch the game while connected to your platform account. The game client checks your digital purchase entitlements upon booting.
2. **80s VHS Filter**: Open **Options > Gameplay > Visual Filters** to toggle the retro VHS visual preset included with Deluxe entitlements.
3. **Weapon Skins & Cosmetics**: Reach the first weapon workbench inside the Scarlet Church compound. Interact with the bench to equip the brass engraved weapon finishes. Check weapon durability and combat stats in our [Weapons Tier List & Durability Guide](/guide/weapons-tier-list-durability/).
4. **Missing Content Troubleshooting**: If owned bonus skins fail to appear, restart the game client to refresh your platform license cache, or verify your game installation files in Steam or your console storage manager.

## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

### Are there any active promo codes for Hellraiser: Revival?
No. Saber Interactive did not implement an in-game promo code redemption feature. Any website claiming to offer working promo code strings for currency, health packs, or weapons is inaccurate.

### Where do I enter codes in Hellraiser: Revival?
There is no text entry box anywhere in the main menu or settings. In-game four-digit numbers are keyed directly into mechanical keypad doors and safe tumblers during gameplay exploration.

### How do I get the Labyrinth Pistol and Martyr Sawblade skins?
Both weapons are pre-order license entitlements. Once installed, they appear automatically in your weapon inventory and workbench customization screen without redeeming a code.

### Will redemption codes be added in future patches?
Developer patch notes through Update 1.1 have not announced or referenced any code redemption feature. If official event drops or promotional vouchers are introduced in future content updates, this guide will document them immediately.
