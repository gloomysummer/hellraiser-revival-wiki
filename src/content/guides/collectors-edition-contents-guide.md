## Hellraiser: Revival Collector's Edition & Pre-Order Breakdown

For hardcore horror collectors and franchise enthusiasts, Saber Interactive and publisher Boss Team Games revealed the limited **Hellraiser: Revival Collector's Edition**. Available across PlayStation 5, Xbox Series X, and PC, this physical boxed set packages exclusive physical memorabilia alongside digital bonuses.

## Physical Collector's Edition Items

The physical edition comes housed in an ornate magnetic-clasp box featuring embossed gold foil sigils:

1. **Die-Cast Genesis Configuration Puzzle Box**: A 1:1 scale metallic functional replica featuring rotating geometric plates and weighted internal core.
2. ***Bleeding Hearts* Novella**: An exclusive 84-page horror novella co-authored by Clive Barker, detailing the origins of the Scarlet Church cult prior to Aidan's arrival.
3. **Embossed SteelBook Case**: Custom artwork showcasing Pinhead and Chatterer in high-gloss lacquer finish.
4. **Art of the Labyrinth Artbook**: A 120-page hardcover volume containing production concept sketches, creature prosthetic photos, and environmental architectural plans.
5. **Numbered Certificate of Authenticity**: Hand-stamped collector's card signed by the creative directors.

## Digital Deluxe Edition Bonuses

Players purchasing either the physical Collector's Edition or the digital Deluxe Edition receive instantaneous entitlement to the following in-game content:

* **80s VHS Aesthetic Filter**: An optional visual filter introducing authentic CRT scanlines, tracking tape static, and saturated neon contrast.
* **Cultist Slayer Melee Skins**: Ornate engraved brass textures for the Tire Iron, Fireaxe, and Sledgehammer.
* **Digital Soundtrack**: High-fidelity FLAC audio files of the complete orchestral dark-ambient game score.
