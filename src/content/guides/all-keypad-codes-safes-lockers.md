## Code Locations in Progression Order

### 1. Red Hallway Cabinet (Code: 9901 / 0199)
* **Location**: Found inside the narrow scarlet corridor shortly after the introductory compound infiltration.
* **Combination**: **`9901`** or **`0199`** (*Community reports record two working variations: test `0199` first, and enter `9901` if the keypad rejects it*).
* **Clue**: Inspect the brass nameplates fixed underneath the two dated oil paintings lining the hallway.
* **Loot**: Gunpowder x1, Wire Coil x1, Bandage x1.

### 2. Medical Room Locker (Code: 9891)
* **Location**: Inside the secondary examination room next to the surgical prep ward.
* **Combination**: **`9891`**
* **Clue**: Examine the arm tattoo etched onto the desiccated corpse submerged in the bloody clawfoot bathtub.
* **Loot**: Gunpowder x1, 27 Coins, Saw Blade x1.

### 3. Graveyard Residence Safe (Code: 9606)
* **Location**: Tucked into the wall alcove inside the dilapidated caretaker residence bordering the graveyard.
* **Combination**: **`9606`**
* **Clue**: Shined with your flashlight directly beneath the framed vintage vinyl record cover hanging on the kitchen partition.
* **Loot**: Pistol Extended Magazine Release attachment, Handgun Ammo x3.

### 4. Bruno's Music Room Cabinet (Code: 1966)
* **Location**: Standing along the wood-paneled wall inside Bruno Keller's personal rehearsal chamber.
* **Combination**: **`1966`**
* **Clue**: The canvas above the organ is dated 1666; an adjacent journal entry instructs adding 300 years to reach the final cipher (1666 + 300 = 1966).
* **Loot**: Handgun Ammo x2, Gunpowder x2, Chemicals x1.

### 5. Snake Room Cabinet (Code: 1952)
* **Location**: Corner locker within the Serpent motif chamber before the inner cloister.
* **Combination**: **`1952`**
* **Clue**: Retrieve the intact archival photograph copy from the adjacent dressing room desk to reveal the year obscured on the burned wall snapshot.
* **Loot**: Revolver Ammo x12, SMG Laser Sight module.

### 6. Private Rooms Washroom Cabinet (Code: 1923)
* **Location**: Mounted adjacent to the frosted glass mirror inside the private residential washroom.
* **Combination**: **`1923`**
* **Clue**: Match the collar insignia numerals worn by the four standing cult figures in the group framed portrait.
* **Loot**: SMG Ammo x7, Revolver Hollow-point rounds, Machete x1, Gunpowder x1.

### 7. Factory Blue Security Door (Code: 1362)
* **Location**: Heavy reinforced blue industrial door blocking access to the central assembly line floor.
* **Combination**: **`1362`**
* **Clue**: Inspect the engraved roman numerals stamped into the plinths of the four decorative sentinel statues encircling the perimeter.
* **Loot**: Bear Trap x1, 52 Coins, Labyrinth Fragment x1, Rifle Ammo x6, Assault Rifle Heavy Lower Handguard.

### 8. Laundry Room Cabinet (Code: 8571)
* **Location**: Metal utility cabinet situated behind the industrial washer drums.
* **Combination**: **`8571`**
* **Clue**: Observe the static flicker across the two wall-mounted surveillance television monitors displaying sequence numbers 85 and 71.
* **Loot**: Skull Crusher melee weapon x1, Bandage x2, Assault Rifle Ammo x30, Revolver +P High-Pressure Ammo, Gunpowder x1.

### 9. Break Room Cabinet (Code: 2342)
* **Location**: Personnel locker nestled within the ruined employee break quarters.
* **Combination**: **`2342`**
* **Clue**: Decipher the numbers painted across the fractured partition wall blasted open by Marlowe; read the digits sequentially from furthest wall to nearest.
* **Loot Breakdown by Source**:
  * *GamesRadar reporting*: Revolver Ammo x6, Assault Rifle Ammo x21, SMG Ammo x28, Revolver Ammo x12.
  * *Gamerblurb reporting*: Revolver Ammo x18, Assault Rifle Ammo x21, SMG Ammo x28.

### 10. Sewer Cabinet (Code: 1512)
* **Location**: Rusted utility lockbox anchored above the drainage culvert inside the lower sewer waterways.
* **Combination**: **`1512`**
* **Clue**: Read the chalk tally board on the culvert masonry recording recorded combat wins and losses.
* **Loot**: Miscellaneous survival ammunition and recovery supplies (exact counts vary depending on current campaign difficulty).

## Codebreaker Achievement

Unlocking keypad containers is not required to complete the story campaign, but opening **any 5 keypad locks** triggers the bronze achievement **Codebreaker**. 

Because locks 1 through 5 appear in early narrative chapters (Red Hallway through Snake Room), thorough players can pop this milestone well before confronting the mid-game Cenobite bosses.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What is the first keypad code in Hellraiser: Revival?
The first locked container is the Red Hallway Cabinet. Community testing confirms two functional combinations: try 0199 first, and if the mechanism remains locked, enter 9901. It contains gunpowder, a wire coil, and a bandage.

### Where do you find safe and locker code clues in the game?
Clues are never random numbers; they are embedded directly within environmental details such as framed painting dates, tally boards, television static displays, corpse tattoos, or architectural statues nearby.

### How do you unlock the Codebreaker achievement?
You earn the Codebreaker achievement immediately after correctly inputting keypad codes into any five separate locked cabinets, safes, or doors across the campaign.

### Does entering an incorrect keypad code trigger an alarm or lock you out?
No. Keypads in Hellraiser: Revival do not trigger defensive lockdown penalties or alert wandering Cenobites upon entering an incorrect code. You can retry combinations freely until the lock disengages.
