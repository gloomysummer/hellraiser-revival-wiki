## Hellraiser: Revival Demo Walkthrough, Safe Codes & Secrets

The public Steam demo for *Clive Barker's Hellraiser: Revival* provides an intense 45-minute preview of the game's exploration, combat weight, and occult puzzle mechanics. Below is the step-by-step walkthrough covering every puzzle, secret room, and critical resource cache.

## Section 1: The Biker Alley & Compound Perimeter

1. **Starting Point**: Aidan begins in a storm-drenched alleyway outside an abandoned motorcycle workshop. 
2. **First Melee Weapon**: Inspect the opened toolbox behind the wrecked Harley to pick up the **Heavy Tire Iron** (Durability: 100%).
3. **Breaching the Gate**: Approach the chained rear door. Use light attack strikes or interact with the padlocked chain using the tire iron to pry it loose.

## Section 2: Entering the Scarlet Church Basement

Inside the basement, power has been cut by the cultists:

* **Restoring Emergency Power**: Navigate past the butchered hanging hogs. Locate the fuse box on the eastern wall. 
* **Missing Fuse Location**: Head into the embalming prep room. Crouch beneath the collapsed iron shelf to discover the **15A Glass Fuse** resting inside an open autoclave tray.
* **Activating the Breaker**: Insert the fuse and pull the two secondary switches in order (Right switch first, then Left).

## Section 3: Safe Code & Chapel Puzzle Solutions

### The Vestry Safe Combination
* **Location**: Found in the church vestry behind the torn painting of Saint Sebastian.
* **Safe Code**: **`0 - 7 - 1 - 9`**
* **Contents**: 1x Box of 12-Gauge Buckshot (4 shells), 1x Emergency Syringe, and the **Acolyte's Journal Note**.

### The First Genesis Configuration Alignment
In the chapel nave, place the puzzle box upon the central obsidian pedestal:
1. Rotate the top brass ring **two notches clockwise** until the interlocking weeping eye sigil aligns with the base.
2. Hold the left trigger to press the locking tab.
3. Align the lower quadrant **one notch counter-clockwise** until the bell chimes.
4. The altar floor will recess, opening the subterranean staircase leading into the demo's final boss encounter chamber.

The box you handle in the demo is the real deal — the [Genesis Configuration puzzle guide](/guide/genesis-configuration-puzzle-guide/) explains every facet and power it unlocks.

## Section 4: Surviving the Chapel Ambush

Upon retrieving the altar key, three Scarlet Church acolytes will breach the stained glass doors:
* **Tactics**: Use the shotgun on the lead charging fanatic to stagger him instantly.
* **Conserving Durability**: Switch back to the tire iron for unarmored followers to save precious ammunition for the full release.
