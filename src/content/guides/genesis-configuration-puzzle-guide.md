## Genesis Configuration Puzzle Box: Solutions & Supernatural Abilities

In *Clive Barker's Hellraiser: Revival*, the iconic puzzle box motif receives a terrifying evolution through the **Genesis Configuration**. Unlike passive inventory items, Aidan Lynch actively wields this mechanical artifact to manipulate dimensional fabric, crack ancient locks, and ward off the Cenobites.

## How the Genesis Configuration Works

The Genesis Configuration is held in first-person view, allowing players to rotate geometric plates, align intricate brass engravings, and trigger occult mechanisms.

### Core Puzzle Phases

1. **Alignment of the Etched Seals**: Rotate the outer ring until the brass serpentine sigils line up with the central ivory core. Listen for auditory clicks indicating correct alignment.
2. **Spring Mechanism Compression**: Depress the opposing circular pins simultaneously to expose the internal brass lattice.
3. **Harmonic Resonance**: Use tactile controller feedback (DualSense haptic triggers) or visual shimmering effects to stop plate rotation at peak harmonic frequency.

As Aidan advances through the Scarlet Church hideout and unlocks deeper configurations, the box rewards the player with extradimensional combat tools:

## Managing Sanity & Corruption

Wielding the Genesis Configuration comes with a severe price. Prolonged usage accelerates Aidan's **Corruption Meter**, warping the screen with auditory hallucinations, bleeding walls, and increasing the probability of summoning Pinhead's immediate presence. Reserve powerful chain attacks for lethal encounters with cult champions and elite foes.
