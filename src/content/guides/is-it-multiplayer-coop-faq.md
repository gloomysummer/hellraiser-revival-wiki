## Is Hellraiser: Revival Multiplayer or Co-op? Modes Explained

A recurring question within the survival horror community is whether *Clive Barker's Hellraiser: Revival* includes cooperative or multiplayer components similar to asymmetric horror titles like *Dead by Daylight* or *The Texas Chain Saw Massacre*.

## The Verdict: 100% Single-Player Campaign

**No, Hellraiser: Revival does not feature multiplayer, co-op, or online PvP.**

**Supported mode: the 1-player story campaign — and only that.** Online co-op, local couch co-op, split-screen and crossplay are all absent (there is no online mode to connect through), so every variant of the question — *is it multiplayer, is there co-op, can I play with friends* — gets the same answer: **no**. Each mode is marked ✅ or ❌ in the table below.

The game is engineered from the ground up as a focused, story-driven single-player survival horror experience. Developer Saber Interactive and author Clive Barker tailored the campaign around Aidan Lynch's solitary descent into madness, ensuring tension and pacing remain strictly controlled without external player interference.

| Game Mode | Supported Status | Details |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Single-Player Story** | ✅ **Full Support** | Narrative campaign starring Aidan Lynch and Pinhead |
| **Online Co-op** | ❌ **Not Supported** | No 2-4 player cooperative progression |
| **Local Couch Co-op** | ❌ **Not Supported** | Single perspective camera only |
| **Asymmetric PvP** | ❌ **Not Supported** | Pinhead cannot be controlled by other human players |

## Why Co-op Was Excluded

In interviews and community Q&As, the design team emphasized that introducing multiplayer would dilute the psychological dread intrinsic to Clive Barker's universe:

1. **Puzzle Box Manipulation**: Operating the Genesis Configuration demands deep mechanical concentration and spatial puzzle-solving that breaks down in chaotic multiplayer environments.
2. **Limited Resource Scarcity**: Survival horror balances fragile weapon durability against relentless Cenobite pursuit. Multiplayer economies disrupt this delicate scarcity balance — [melee weapons wear out with use](/guide/weapons-tier-list-durability/), so durable tools have to be rationed across the group.
3. **Pacing and Atmosphere**: Exploring the Scarlet Church and the subterranean Labyrinth requires solitary immersion to evoke genuine terror.

Playing alone changes the risk math — the [demo walkthrough secrets](/guide/demo-walkthrough-secrets/) include solo-safe routes through the Scarlet Church.

## Will Multiplayer Be Added in Future DLC?

Saber Interactive has indicated no plans for multiplayer post-launch expansions. Post-launch support will focus on performance updates and potential single-player narrative chapters expanding on the *Bleeding Hearts* novella lore.
