## Bruno's Scalpel: What It Actually Is

In *Clive Barker's Hellraiser: Revival*, **Bruno's Scalpel** is the **Treasure (valuable) item** that boss **Bruno Keller** leaves behind when you banish him during the boss fight in the basement of the cult compound. It is **not an equipable weapon**: you cannot add it to your loadout, swing it at enemies, or repair it. Its only purpose in the game economy is to be **sold to the merchant Zora Beckett** for coins.

This dossier serves as the definitive reference for the scalpel's exact sale value, vendor pricing comparisons, pickup timing, and arena recovery risks.

---

## Quick Answer: How to Get Bruno's Scalpel & What to Do With It

* **What it is**: A Treasure-class valuable dropped by Bruno Keller — a sellable collectible, not gear.
* **How to get it**: Beat the Bruno boss fight, interact at the blue glow to **banish** him, wait 2–3 seconds for the corrosive blood pool to settle, then pick the scalpel up off the floor where his remains bubble.
* **What it does in combat**: Nothing. It cannot be equipped or used as a weapon, and it has no durability stats.
* **What to do with it**: Sell it to **Zora Beckett** at her next shop for coins — the standard fate of every "Treasure"-marked item.
* **Do not leave it behind**: Walkthroughs flag it as an easy miss before you move on to the Statue's Missing Part objective, so grab it during the fight wrap-up.

## Vendor Economy: Sale Value to Zora Beckett

When brought to the merchant **Zora Beckett**, Bruno's Scalpel yields a substantial early-game windfall:

* **Zora Beckett Sale Price**: **450–500 coins** *(Community-reported baseline is 450 coins; small variations up to 500 coins depend on difficulty preset and merchant negotiation flags)*.
* **Purchasing Power**: Selling the scalpel immediately funds 2 full batches of shotgun shells, 1 revolver ammo recipe craft, or 2 lockpicks needed for compound armory lockers.

## Repeatability & Playthrough Limits

* **Single Acquisition per Campaign**: Bruno's Scalpel can only be looted **once per playthrough**.
* **No Respawn Mechanism**: Bruno Keller is a scripted story encounter in the Dissection Gallery. Once defeated and banished, the arena remains cleared; revisiting the basement later will not trigger a respawn or generate duplicate scalpel drops.
* **New Game Plus & Replay**: Collecting an additional Bruno's Scalpel requires starting a fresh campaign save or transitioning into New Game Plus after completing the story.

## Route Risks & Looting Timing in the Boss Arena

While looting the scalpel is straightforward, multiple players report missing the item or taking unnecessary hazard damage during the post-fight transition:

1. **Corrosive Viscera Hazard (Wait 2–3 Seconds)**:
   * When Bruno Keller enters his blue stagger state and you trigger the banishment interaction, his physical form collapses into a foaming puddle of dark viscera and embalming acid.
   * Rushing into the pool immediately triggers an environmental acid tick, dealing minor vitality damage. Wait 2 to 3 seconds for the bubble animation to settle before stepping into the center to collect the scalpel.
2. **Point-of-No-Return Lockout (Do Not Rush the Exit)**:
   * The southern doorway leads into the drainage corridor toward the *Statue's Missing Part* quest objective.
   * Passing through the threshold and activating the mechanical lever triggers a cutscene and a barred iron gate that permanently locks the Dissection Gallery behind you for that chapter. Always pick up the scalpel from the floor before interacting with the transition door.
3. **Elevator Hallway Stash Recovery**:
   * If you followed standard boss strategy and dropped spare blunt tools (such as the Heavy Pipe or Tire Iron) in the elevator corridor to avoid encumbrance or mid-fight clutter, retrieve those stored tools immediately after securing the scalpel before proceeding to Zora's trading post.

## Fact-Check Note: The "Bone-Saw Scalpel" Misconception

Earlier community posts and draft guides described an equipable "Bone-Saw Scalpel" B-tier one-handed blade with 12 durability hits and silent stealth executions. That description was **incorrect** and has been thoroughly debunked:

* No official patch note or developer documentation lists a weapon named Bone-Saw Scalpel; Saber Interactive's published notes never mention it.
* In-game playthroughs and community speedruns confirm that Keller drops a single floor collectible classified as **Treasure**.
* The game's actual bladed melee weapons are the **Machete**, **Cleaver**, and **Jagged Messer** (purchased from Zora), plus Aidan's unbreakable starting knife. Wieldable durability and stagger rankings are catalogued in our [weapons tier list](/guide/weapons-tier-list-durability/).

## Bruno's Scalpel FAQ

### Can you use Bruno's Scalpel as a weapon?

No. Bruno's Scalpel is a Treasure item with no combat use. It cannot be equipped, does not appear in your weapon wheel, and has no durability or damage values. If you want a blade for combat, wield the Machete, Cleaver, or Aidan's starting knife.

### How do you get Bruno's Scalpel?

Defeat Bruno Keller in the boss fight in the basement of the cult compound, interact at the blue glow to banish him, wait 2–3 seconds for the acid bubbles to settle, then pick the scalpel up off the arena floor.

### How much is Bruno's Scalpel worth?

When sold to the merchant Zora Beckett, Bruno's Scalpel yields **450–500 coins** *(Community-reported)*, making it one of the largest single-item financial windfalls in the first half of the game.

### Is Bruno's Scalpel missable?

Yes. It stays on the arena floor after the fight rather than being auto-added to your inventory. If you leave through the iron exit gate toward the Statue's Missing Part objective without looting it, the gate locks and you cannot re-enter the Dissection Gallery.

### Does Bruno's Scalpel have durability or a tier rating?

No. Durability and tier ratings apply strictly to weapons you swing. Bruno's Scalpel is a sellable collectible; for actual melee durability values, consult our [weapons tier list and durability guide](/guide/weapons-tier-list-durability/).

### Who is Bruno Keller?

Bruno Keller is the Scarlet Church's surgeon-prophet — known as the "Picasso of the Scalpel" — and the primary human boss of the basement compound. His full phase tactics, armor stagger methods, and weak points are covered in the [Bruno Keller boss guide](/guide/bruno-keller-boss-guide/).
