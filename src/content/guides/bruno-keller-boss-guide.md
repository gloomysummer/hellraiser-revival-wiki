## How to Beat Bruno Keller (The Picasso of the Scalpel)

Within the fanatical hierarchy of the **Scarlet Church**, few figures are as terrifying as **Bruno Keller**. Revering bodily mutilation as sacred art, Keller stalks the player through a labyrinth of hanging flesh hooks, operating tables, and blood-soaked dissection wards.

## Boss Profile: Bruno Keller

* **Arena**: The Dissection Gallery (Scarlet Church Sub-level 2)
* **Primary Weapons**: Dual Bone-Saw Scalpels & Embalming Fluid Syringes
* **Damage Type**: Fast Piercing Melee & Stacking Bleed Damage
* **Recommended Weaponry**: Heavy Pipe / Sledgehammer (Blunt Armor Break), Shotgun (Close Range Stagger)

## Phase 1: Operating Room Stalker

During the opening phase, Keller relies on stealth and sudden lunges from behind surgical curtains.

1. **Keep Your Back to Stone Walls**: Avoid standing beneath hanging carcasses; Keller can hook chains to trigger environmental crush traps.
2. **Bait the Three-Slash Combo**: Wait for Keller to telegraph his three-hit scalpel slash. Step back on the second swing, then counter-attack with a heavy blunt overhead strike to crack his leather surgical harness.
3. **Bleed Management**: If struck by his bone-saw, immediately consume a Tourniquet or Bandage to halt hemorrhage before your health bar degrades completely.

Before the fight, sanity-check your loadout against the [weapons tier list and durability guide](/guide/weapons-tier-list-durability/) — broken tools mid-boss are a run-killer.

## Phase 2: Berserk Frenzy & Syringe Throwing

Once reduced below 50% health, Keller douses his scalpels in adrenaline and retreats to elevated balconies.

* **Dodge Acidic Injections**: Keller will lob embalming syringes across the room. Roll laterally behind metal surgical trays to absorb the projectiles.
* **Exploiting the Genesis Configuration**: Activate **Spectral Insight** to detect his thermal footprint before he drops down. When he leaps, trigger **Kinetic Repulsion** or a shotgun blast mid-air to send him into a vulnerable knocked-down state.
* **Finishing the Fight**: Focus heavy blunt strikes on his unprotected head once his chest armor breaks. Deliver the final execution strike before he can crawl toward the emergency blood valve.

## Key Encounter Rewards

Defeating Bruno Keller unlocks the **Dissection Key**, access to the embalming wing's weapon upgrade bench, and drops rare crafting materials for upgraded trauma kits.

This fight is one encounter in a larger roster — the [all Cenobites weaknesses guide](/guide/all-cenobites-weaknesses-guide/) covers every entity's evasion patterns.
