Combat in *Clive Barker's Hellraiser: Revival* is brutal, tactile, and deliberately unforgiving. Weapons degrade with every swing, and enemies possess distinct armor layers requiring players to switch strategically between **Blunt**, **Sharp**, and **Firearm** arsenals.

## Weapon Durability & Repair Mechanics

Every melee hit directly degrades your weapon's structural integrity:
1. **White Condition (100% - 50%)**: Full base damage, maximum swing speed, and reliable stagger frames.
2. **Yellow Condition (49% - 15%)**: 20% reduced damage; weapons emit rattling metallic audio upon impact.
3. **Red Condition (< 15%)**: Critical structural fracture. The next heavy attack has a 50% probability of shattering the weapon completely into useless scrap iron.

### Workbench Maintenance & Field Durability Preservation

Scattered throughout the Scarlet Church compound are maintenance workbenches and temporary salvage stations. Players can consume **Scrap Metal** and **Grindstone Oil** to restore weapon condition back to pristine status before challenging boss territories. Spare scrap materials and high-tier ammunition can frequently be scavenged from locked caches detailed in our [all keypad and safe codes guide](/guide/all-keypad-codes-safes-lockers/) and our [codes archive](/codes/).

To avoid running completely empty-handed during early exploration:
* **Reserve Heavy Blunt Weapons for Armored Foes**: Do not burn Crowbar or Tire Iron durability on wooden crates or fragile environment barricades; use your bare hands or kick mechanics where prompt icons appear.
* **Avoid Striking Stone & Metal Surfaces**: Missing an enemy and striking masonry or steel doors inflicts double durability degradation. Keep your swings centered when fighting inside narrow church hallways.
* **Stagger with Genesis Repulsion**: When facing swarming Cultist Runners, lead with **Kinetic Repulsion** or dimensional shockwaves to stagger enemies without expending weapon durability hits.

### The Scrap Economy: Crafting Allocation vs. Repair Decisions

Scrap Metal acts as the most tightly constrained resource throughout the campaign, creating a dual tax on survival: every unit allocated to repairing a low-tier pipe or crowbar directly deprives players of crafting vital medical tourniquets or high-grade ammunition.

To maximize your Scrap Metal efficiency:
* **Never Swing in Red Condition (< 15%)**: A broken tool yields only negligible scrap salvage. If a high-tier weapon reaches red condition, store it in your inventory until reaching a workbench with Grindstone Oil rather than risking an outright shatter.
* **Lead with Kinetic Crowd Control**: Use the Genesis Configuration's **Kinetic Repulsion** ability to stagger or knock down swarming cultist groups for zero durability expenditure, then close in with clean executions.
* **Prioritize High-Yield Armory Caches**: Keypad-locked lockers often hold pre-fabricated weapon attachments and pristine tools. Consult our [all keypad and safe codes directory](/guide/all-keypad-codes-safes-lockers/) to bypass crafting taxes entirely.

## Armor Penetration vs. Flesh Damage Rule

* **Blunt Weapons (Hammers, Pipes, Crowbars)**: Deliver concussive force through leather vests, bone armor, and steel plates. Always use blunt tools first to strip enemy armor before finishing with sharp blades.
* **Sharp Weapons (Machetes, Scalpels, Axes)**: Deal extreme bleed and dismemberment damage against exposed flesh, but suffer triple durability penalty when striking armored targets.

### Enemy-Specific Combat Matching Protocols

* **Demonic Entities (Limb Dismemberment)**: Slicing blades such as the Rusty Fireaxe or [Bruno's Scalpel](/guide/brunos-scalpel-weapon-guide/) sever demonic limbs rapidly, cancelling lunging grabs and reducing crawl speeds.
* **Human Cultists (Ranged Firearm Control)**: Human cultists lack supernatural posture regeneration; eliminate ranged snipers and patrol guards with handgun headshots to preserve melee tool durability.
* **Armored Enforcers (Faceplate Shatter Protocol)**: Enforcers wearing scrap iron masks deflect pistol rounds entirely. Lead with an overhead blunt strike (Heavy Sledgehammer or Tire Iron) to shatter the faceplate, then finish with a firearm headshot.
* **Firearm Stagger to Protect Weapon Integrity**: Discharging a firearm round into a charging enemy triggers an immediate stagger frame, providing a safe window for follow-up melee executions without absorbing counter-damage.
