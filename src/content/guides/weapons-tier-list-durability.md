## Weapons Tier List & Durability Management Guide

Combat in *Clive Barker's Hellraiser: Revival* is brutal, tactile, and deliberately unforgiving. Weapons degrade with every swing, and enemies possess distinct armor layers requiring players to switch strategically between **Blunt**, **Sharp**, and **Firearm** arsenals.

## Weapon Durability & Repair Mechanics

Every melee hit directly degrades your weapon's structural integrity:
1. **White Condition (100% - 50%)**: Full base damage, maximum swing speed, and reliable stagger frames.
2. **Yellow Condition (49% - 15%)**: 20% reduced damage; weapons emit rattling metallic audio upon impact.
3. **Red Condition (< 15%)**: Critical structural fracture. The next heavy attack has a 50% probability of shattering the weapon completely into useless scrap iron.

### Workbench Maintenance & Sharpening Kits
Scattered throughout the Scarlet Church compound are maintenance workbenches. Players can consume **Scrap Metal** and **Grindstone Oil** to restore weapon condition back to pristine status before challenging boss territories.

## Armor Penetration vs. Flesh Damage Rule

* **Blunt Weapons (Hammers, Pipes, Crowbars)**: Deliver concussive force through leather vests, bone armor, and steel plates. Always use blunt tools first to strip enemy armor before finishing with sharp blades.
* **Sharp Weapons (Machetes, Scalpels, Axes)**: Deal extreme bleed and dismemberment damage against exposed flesh, but suffer triple durability penalty when striking armored targets.
