## Hellraiser: Revival Release Date — Confirmed Facts

The launch window is no longer a moving target. Here is what is confirmed for **Clive Barker's Hellraiser: Revival**:

* **Release date:** October 8, 2026 — confirmed on the [official Steam store page](https://store.steampowered.com/app/1551980).
* **Platforms:** PC via Steam, PlayStation 5, Xbox Series X|S, with a Nintendo Switch 2 release announced for the same date.
* **Edition tiers:** a Standard Edition and a Deluxe Edition ship at launch, plus physical Collector's Editions sold through Boss Team Games — the [Collector's Edition contents guide](/guide/collectors-edition-contents-guide/) breaks down every item.
* **Pre-order incentives:** the Labyrinth Pistol Skin and Martyr Sawblade Skin are entitlement-based and apply at release — they are tracked in the [October 2026 codes ledger](/codes/october-2026/).

## Does Steam Unlock at the Same Time?

Saber Interactive has not published a separate Steam unlock hour, and Steam does not list one on the store page. Two practical consequences:

1. **Do not repost the ET time as a Steam time.** If your region's Steam counter says something different, the counter is the authority for your account.
2. **The Steam page is the live source.** A countdown appears there once the store flips the game from pre-release to released — that change is the unlock signal, not a third-party table.

## What Is Still Unconfirmed

Consistent with this wiki's evidence policy, these items are **not** asserted anywhere on this page because no first-party statement exists yet:

* Preload windows for any platform.
* Day-one patch size and patch notes — a patch-notes page is only worth publishing once the publisher ships the text.
* Trophy and achievement lists — these appear at unlock, not before.
* Any unlock hour that contradicts the storefront that sold you the license.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What time does Hellraiser: Revival come out?

The PlayStation Store listing indicates 9:00 A.M. ET / 6:00 A.M. PT on October 8, 2026 — 2:00 P.M. in the UK and 3:00 P.M. in Central Europe. Steam has not published a separate hour.

### Is Hellraiser: Revival out on October 8 everywhere?

The date is global, but local clock time is not. New Zealand, for example, crosses into October 9 because of the timezone offset.

### Can I preload Hellraiser: Revival?

No preload window has been announced for any platform. Treat any exact preload schedule you see elsewhere as unconfirmed until a first-party source publishes it.
