## All Cenobites Encounters, Behaviors & Evasion Tactics

In *Clive Barker's Hellraiser: Revival*, the demonic theologians known as the **Cenobites (The Order of the Gash)** serve as apex stalker entities. Governed by cold, cosmic detachment, these creatures cannot be killed by conventional bullets or blades; surviving them requires tactical evasion, spatial awareness, and exploiting their specific sensory fixations.

## 1. Pinhead (The Hell Priest)

Voiced by franchise icon **Doug Bradley**, Pinhead orchestrates the horrors of the Labyrinth and commands the hooked chains that bind sinners.

* **Behavior**: Pinhead rarely sprints. Instead, he teleports behind dimensional rifts, altering gravity and manipulating metallic architecture.
* **Primary Threat**: Summoned spiked chains that pin Aidan to the ground, requiring rapid QTE button inputs to tear free before suffering fatal lacerations.
* **Evasion Strategy**: Never maintain unbroken line-of-sight across wide open halls. Break visual tracking by cutting corners into narrow stone doorways where chain trajectories are obstructed. Use the Genesis Configuration's **Inversion Wave** to dispel active rifts.

## 2. Chatterer

Distinguishable by his constantly gnashing, exposed teeth and stitched eyelids, Chatterer is the most aggressively mobile Cenobite in the game.

* **Sensory Mechanism**: Fully blind. Tracks Aidan exclusively through **audio vibrations** and surface footsteps.
* **Weakness**: Glass bottles and metallic debris scattered across the floor can be thrown in opposite directions to misdirect his high-speed charges.
* **Combat Interaction**: Crouched stealth movement completely neutralizes his tracking. Avoid walking over broken porcelain or spilled liquid pools.

## 3. Butterball

A grotesque, rotund figure donning dark sunglasses, Butterball embodies gluttony and unstoppable physical momentum.

* **Behavior**: Slow-moving juggernaut capable of shattering wooden barricades and stone pillars with brute force.
* **Weakness**: Extremely poor turning radius. Bait his forward bull rush into heavy stone pillars to induce prolonged stagger windows, granting ample time to activate gate levers.

## 4. Deepthroat (The Female Cenobite)

Characterized by wires pulling open the flesh of her throat, she acts as a psychological stalker in upper vaulted chambers.

* **Behavior**: Employs sonic screeches that invert Aidan's camera controls and deplete stamina reserves.
* **Evasion Strategy**: Equip acoustic ear plugs found in cultist storage boxes and utilize smoke grenades to disrupt her dimensional gaze.
