## Hellraiser: Revival Codes & Bonus Redemption Ledger

This ledger tracks all verified developer promotional gifts, community redemption codes, and digital content entitlements for *Clive Barker's Hellraiser: Revival*. We actively monitor official community channels to filter out deceptive code generators.

## Current Promo Codes Status

**The game is out now** — Saber Interactive confirmed the global release on October 8, 2026, and there is still **no text-based promo code** for Hellraiser: Revival. Every cosmetic, the artbook, and the *Bleeding Hearts* digital novella arrive as platform store **entitlements** attached to your account, not as strings you type into a menu.

That matters for how you search: any site showing a "code list" for this game with working strings is either recycling another game's codes or generating them. The launch-day unlock schedule itself is covered in the [release time and unlock times guide](/guide/release-time-unlock-times-guide/).

If you are searching for **in-game puzzle combinations, locker codes, and safe codes** to open locked containers across the campaign, refer directly to our full guide: [Hellraiser Revival All Codes: Safes & Lockers](/guide/all-keypad-codes-safes-lockers/).

## How Deluxe & Pre-Order Rewards Unlock

1. **Install and launch the game**: the release is live on PC via Steam, PlayStation 5, Xbox Series X|S and Nintendo Switch 2 — the entitlement shows up as soon as your account holds the license.
2. **Check your platform library**, not an in-game redeem box: on Steam the extras appear as owned DLC; on PlayStation and Xbox they are tied to the edition you bought.
3. **Access the Options Menu**: navigate to **Options > Gameplay > Visual Filters** to activate the *80s VHS Mode* granted by the Deluxe or Collector's Edition license.
4. **Weapon Workbench**: when you reach your first workbench inside the Scarlet Church compound, interact with weapon customization to equip the brass engraved skins.

## Pre-Order Skins You Should Already Own

Players who pre-ordered any edition receive the **Labyrinth Pistol Skin** and **Martyr Sawblade Skin** — publisher materials confirm both are applied at release rather than redeemed with a code. If your account shows the purchase but the skins are missing in-game, it is an entitlement sync issue, not a missing code: restart the client so it re-checks the license.

The physical Collector's Editions bundle the same digital content on top of licensed collectibles — the [Collector's Edition contents guide](/guide/collectors-edition-contents-guide/) lists every item so you can confirm what your order includes.
